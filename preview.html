Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AD Craft.

What is AD Craft?

AD Craft is an AI-powered real-estate lead automation platform that responds to, qualifies and follows up with prospects and helps schedule site visits.

Does it work with WhatsApp?

Yes. The platform is designed to integrate directly with WhatsApp-based lead conversations via the official Meta Cloud API.

Can the AI answer property-specific questions?

Yes. Property brochures, FAQs, pricing information and other approved project information can be used as the AI's knowledge source.

Can it book site visits?

Yes. Calendar integrations (Google Calendar / Calendly) can be used to schedule appointments and dispatch Google Maps pins.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. The target experience is self-serve onboarding. Connect your WhatsApp, upload your PDF brochures, and start in 15 minutes.

Can I use it for multiple properties?

Yes, depending on the subscription plan and configuration (Growth supports up to 5 properties, Global supports unlimited).

Does AD Craft replace my sales team?

No. It handles repetitive lead-response and qualification work so the sales team can focus on high-intent prospects and closing.
